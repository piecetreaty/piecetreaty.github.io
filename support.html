Support

  • Restore your purchase: open your suitcase (bottom right) → More → Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.
  • Moving to a new iPhone: turn on iCloud for Piece Treaty. Empty save slots on the new device show a ☁ button to restore.
  • Purchase waiting for approval (Ask to Buy): the unlock appears automatically as soon as it's approved.
  • Too hard or too easy? Opponents adjust to how you play: win a few and they get tougher, lose a few and they ease off.
  • Don't want a piece following you? Suitcase → More → Settings → Piece follower.
