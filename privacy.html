Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Piece Treaty is a single-player chess adventure. We don't collect or store any personal data. The only thing that ever leaves your device is a chess.com username, and only if you choose to enter one.

  • No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics code.
  • Your saves are stored on your device. On iPhone, if you use iCloud, a backup copy is kept in your own iCloud storage, which Apple operates. On Android, if you've turned on Google backup, your phone's own backup can include them, which Google operates. We can't access either.
  • Game Center (iPhone only) achievements and leaderboards are provided by Apple under your Game Center account and Apple's privacy policy.
  • chess.com (optional). When you start a game you can enter your chess.com username. If you do, the app sends that username to chess.com's public API to read your public ratings, and makes your opponents a better match for you. chess.com will see your device's IP address, as with any web request, and its own privacy policy applies. We don't run a server and never receive your username: it's kept only in your save on your device (and your iCloud or Google backup, if you use one). You can skip this step, and no password is ever asked for.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store, or by Google through Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • Children: the game collects no data from anyone, including children. The chess.com step asks only for a public username, and can be skipped.

Questions: open an issue on the Piece Treaty support page.

Piece Treaty is an independent game. Not affiliated with chess.com.
