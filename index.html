A chess story of old New York

New York, the 1930s. The old Families settle every quarrel the same way: one game of chess. Peace at the table, pieces on the board.

You're Vinnie (or Lucia) Pedone, fresh off the boat from Palermo with eight pawns and your Nonno's brass chess clock. Win his chess set back from the Scacchi Family, one boss and one borough at a time.
